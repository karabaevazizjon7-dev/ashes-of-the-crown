# Visual asset direction — Ashes of the Crown

Existing art is retained: `estervale_book_art.png`, location paintings for castle/harbour/market/garden, and portraits for Garrett, Thomas, Reynar, Celeste, Mira and Adrian.

New illustrations should be commissioned or generated as individual scene assets, not as a contact sheet. Keep a consistent grounded medieval-fantasy look: realistic materials, restrained muted palette, natural cinematic lighting, believable clothing and architecture, no modern objects, no text or logos embedded in the artwork.

Suggested first expansion set (30–50 files):
- 8 environment paintings: royal keep exterior, council hall, archive, armory tower, old chapel, bridge at night, village street, forest road.
- 8 character portraits: existing six characters in consistent lighting plus two supporting characters introduced by the story.
- 6 political scenes: royal council, private audience, sealed-letter exchange, night meeting, court accusation, coronation preparations.
- 6 travel/nature scenes: river crossing, autumn woodland, mountain pass, winter garden, storm coast, rural fields.
- 6 conflict scenes: guard patrol, ambush aftermath, siege preparations, duel, battlefield before combat, victory/defeat aftermath.

All artwork must be mapped to actual scene/location keys in the game; do not replace existing assets or alter story continuity merely to fit an image.
