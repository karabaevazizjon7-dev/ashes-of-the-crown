# Music candidates for Ashes of the Crown

The following tracks/pages were checked for explicit reuse terms. They are candidates only; their audio binaries are **not** included in V86 because external downloads were unavailable in the build environment.

## CC0 — suitable for commercial and offline game distribution

- RandomMind, **Medieval: Exploration** — CC0; menu/exploration. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-exploration
- RandomMind, **Medieval: Harvest Season** — CC0; market/tavern/settlement. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-harvest-season
- RandomMind, **Medieval: Victory Theme** — CC0; victory stinger. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-victory-theme
- RandomMind, **Medieval: The Bard's Tale** — CC0; town/castle/tavern. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-bards-tale
- RandomMind, **Medieval: Battle** — CC0; combat. Listed in the author's CC0 music collection: https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-audio-uploader-randommind

## Attribution / terms to preserve if used

CC0 does not require attribution, but retain creator and source information in the project's credits as a courtesy. Verify the individual asset page and download file before shipping. Do not hotlink or stream these files at runtime: bundle approved audio locally so the game remains fully offline.
